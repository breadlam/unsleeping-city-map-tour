# The Unsleeping City: walking tours

**Live tours: https://breadlam.github.io/unsleeping-city-map-tour/walking-tour/**

Self-guided tours of the real New York places from Dimension 20's *The Unsleeping City*, one per chapter.
Each tour is two full days of walking with a few subway rides, so it fills a weekend; either day also works on its own.
Each chapter also lists optional extra stops for a longer weekend.

| Tour | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Chapter 1** (December) | *The ferry to Times Square*: Staten Island Ferry, Wall St & the Stock Exchange, City Hall, Tompkins Square Park, Union Square, Empire State Building, NY Public Library, Grand Central, Times Square. 9 stops, 8.5 km on foot, 1 subway ride. | *Central Park, Harlem and the L to Nod*: Central Park South, Bethesda Fountain, the Ramble & Belvedere Castle, the Met, the Reservoir, 125th St, Clinton Hill, Lorimer St station. 8 stops, 7.5 km, 3 rides. |
| **Chapter 2** (autumn) | *The Dragon's downtown*: Battery Park, Ellis Island, 9/11 Memorial, City Hall & the old City Hall station, Chinatown, the Holland Tunnel & the Hudson, West 4th St. 7 stops, 6.3 km, ferries only. | *Midtown to the Hoard*: Herald Square, Empire State Building, NY Public Library, Grand Central, W 72nd St, the Central Park Carousel, the Met, 110th St, 125th St. 9 stops, 9.3 km, 2 rides. |

Every stop says what happens there in the story (with episode numbers and links to the scenes on the story map),
what you can actually see today, the nearest subway stations, and how to get to the next stop.

## Publishing

The tours are published from the same repository as the story map
([breadlam/unsleeping-city-map-tour](https://github.com/breadlam/unsleeping-city-map-tour)), in its `walking-tour/` folder.
GitHub Pages serves every folder at its own address, so the two sites stay separate:
the story map is at the site root, and the tours are at `/walking-tour/`. To update the tours, copy this folder's files into
`walking-tour/` in that repository and push. The existing Pages setting (Deploy from a branch, `main`, `/ (root)`) covers both.

To host the tours on their own instead, upload this folder to the root of any repository (with `.nojekyll`) and turn on Pages there.

The scene links point at the story map's address, set as `STORY_MAP_URL` in `tour_def.py`; rebuild if it moves.

Opening `index.html` straight from the folder also works. The map tiles need an internet connection.

## How the routes were made

- **Walking** legs are routed along streets and park paths by [OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/)'s foot profile
  (hosted by [FOSSGIS](https://fossgis.de/)) on © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) data.
  Times assume 4.8 km/h.
- **Subway** rides follow the MTA's track geometry for that line between the two stations (from [data.ny.gov](https://data.ny.gov/)).
  Times assume about 27 km/h including stops, plus 5 minutes' wait. Stop counts are for the first line listed.
  Station accessibility comes from the same MTA dataset.
- **Ferries** follow the real ferry lines mapped in OpenStreetMap, with routed walks to and from the piers.
- Nothing is drawn as a straight line. Walks are kept off ferries, and a change of line inside a station is drawn as part of the subway trip.
- "About N hours with visits" adds a suggested time at each stop to the travel time.

Real-world details are kept to things that change rarely. Check opening hours, tickets (Ellis Island, the Met, the Empire State
Building and the 9/11 Museum are ticketed) and weekend subway service changes before you go.

## Rebuilding

From the `Location Timeline` folder (needs network access for OSRM, and the MTA `service_lines.json` / `stations.json` from data.ny.gov):

```bash
python3 source/scripts/build_tour.py events.csv <mta_dir> <osrm_cache_dir> <work_dir> source/data/osm_ferry_routes.json   # writes tour_data.js, KML, GeoJSON, tour_routes.json
python3 source/scripts/render_tour.py <work_dir>/tour_routes.json <work_dir>/tour_data.js <tile_cache_dir> walking_tour/maps   # needs matplotlib, pillow, numpy
```

The tours themselves (stops, text, which legs take the subway) are defined in `source/scripts/tour_def.py`.
