The Unsleeping City

The real New York places from Dimension 20's The Unsleeping City, on foot and by subway. One tour per chapter; each takes a weekend, or pick one day.

Take it with you: Google My Maps file (KML) · GeoJSON · printable map of this day

Fan-made and unofficial. The Unsleeping City belongs to Dropout / Dimension 20. Story details come from fan transcripts. Times are estimates; check opening hours, tickets and weekend subway changes before you go.

Walking routes: OSRM (hosted by FOSSGIS) on OpenStreetMap data. Subway track and stations: MTA via data.ny.gov. Map: Leaflet, Esri and OpenStreetMap tiles.
